The court noted that a game on the stock market does not release a professional participant from the duty to disclose risk. Here the word game was used by one side of the dispute, but legal evaluation requires a precise description of the transaction.

The rules of a sports tournament define when a game is considered complete and how a violation of the regulations is recorded. For the committee, the game is a procedure with a verifiable result.

The justice of a court proceeding includes the right to be heard, equal access to evidence, and a reasoned decision. Violation of one guarantee can cast doubt on the whole process.

Constitutional justice does not always coincide with arithmetic equality. A law may account for the vulnerability of a group if the distinction aims to restore a real balance.

In a labor dispute, the justice of compensation is assessed with regard to harm, the conduct of the parties, and the duration of the violation. The court is not required to award the same amount in different circumstances.

A transport knot is recognized as infrastructure if regular route intersections are organized through it. The legal status of such a knot affects safety, leasing, and operator liability.

In a supply contract, a logistic knot marks the place where risk is transferred and goods are inspected. An error in describing the knot can change the moment when an obligation is fulfilled.

A land dispute arose because of a knot of utility networks located on the boundary between parcels. An expert examination must determine who owns the knot and who must maintain the communications.

The notary pointed to a knot of mutual obligations: debt, pledge, and guarantee were connected so that ending one condition changed the others. This knot could not be untied by a simple receipt.
