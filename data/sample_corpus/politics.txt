The psychologist wrote about the struggle with addiction as a long series of small decisions. In that struggle there is no single enemy, because habit is embedded in schedule, body, and relationships.

The doctor said the body's struggle with infection takes time. Medical struggle here means an immune response, not a person's act of will.

The lawyer described the struggle against corruption as a system of procedures: disclosure of interests, procurement audits, and witness protection. In legal language, struggle becomes a set of institutions.

Forum users argued about whether a struggle for justice can help when the same group moderates the rules. For participants, struggle was both a moral position and a way not to lose their voice.

In the novel, the heroine grew tired of struggling with her family and the city. Her struggle became quiet: she stopped justifying herself and began to live as if permission had already been granted.

Economists described the struggle of firms for a market through prices, patents, and logistics. Such struggle resembles a game of strategies, but its consequences exceed the model.

In the environmental movement, the struggle for the river united scientists, fishers, and schoolchildren. Each group saw a different reason, but the word struggle held the varied coalition together.
